## Reply (optional)

```
The pointer helper is C source against wlr-virtual-pointer-unstable-v1.
Catalog rule 17 means no committed binaries: `make -C ~/.hermes/plugins/hyprland/pointer`.

Modifier+click is the one limitation: holding a key across a pointer event
needs /dev/uinput, which this provider avoids. `key` plus a plain click covers
most shortcuts.
```
